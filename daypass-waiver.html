Day Pass Waiver

Welcome to Fit Pit! Because a day pass gives you full use of our gym, we ask every visitor to read and sign this short waiver before their first workout. Please read it carefully — by signing, you agree to the terms below.

Day Pass Visitor Waiver & Liability Release

Fit Pit Zanzibar · Zanzibar Sporting Club, Cairo/Kiwengwa

1. Health & Fitness Declaration

  • I confirm that I am in good general health and able to take part in physical exercise.
  • I am not aware of any medical condition, injury, pregnancy, or other reason that makes it unsafe for me to exercise. If I am in any doubt, I understand I should consult a doctor first, and that I take part at my own risk.
  • I will stop exercising and tell a member of staff if I feel unwell at any point during my visit.
  • I confirm that I am at least 17 years old, the minimum age for entry to Fit Pit.

2. Assumption of Risk & Release of Liability

  • I understand that using gym equipment and taking part in exercise, fitness classes, or personal training carries inherent risks, including the risk of injury.
  • I take part voluntarily and accept full responsibility for these risks.
  • I confirm that I am experienced with gym equipment and know how to use all of the equipment safely and correctly on my own. I do not require, and am not relying on, any instruction, supervision, or training from Fit Pit staff, and I use the equipment at my own risk.
  • To the fullest extent permitted by law, I release Fit Pit Zanzibar, its owners, staff, and trainers from any liability for injury, accident, loss, or health issue arising from my use of the facilities or participation in any activity — except to the extent it is caused by the gross negligence or wilful misconduct of Fit Pit or its staff.
  • Fit Pit is not responsible for loss of, or damage to, personal belongings brought onto the premises.

3. Gym Rules & Conduct

I agree to follow these rules and any reasonable instruction given by staff:

  • Attire: Only proper gym wear with athletic trainers is permitted. Flip-flops, sandals, beachwear, and training without a shirt are not allowed.
  • Equipment: Re-rack weights and return equipment after use. I am liable for the cost of any damage I cause through inappropriate use of equipment.
  • Lockers & belongings: Store personal items in the lockers provided. A $10 USD fee applies for a lost locker key. Fit Pit is not liable for items left in lockers.
  • Food & drink: Only bottled water or sports drinks are allowed in the gym. Please dispose of any litter appropriately.
  • Photography: Progress selfies are welcome, but please don't film or photograph other members without their consent. Commercial photography needs prior permission.
  • Respect: Treat other members and staff with courtesy at all times. Harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful behaviour will not be tolerated and may result in being asked to leave without a refund.
  • Music: Gym music is set by Fit Pit; please use personal earphones if you'd prefer something different.
  • Right of admission: Fit Pit management and staff reserve the right to refuse admission, or to ask a visitor to leave the premises, at their discretion — for example for unsafe or disruptive behaviour, intoxication, or a breach of these rules. Day pass fees are non-refundable in such cases.

4. Day Pass Terms

  • A day pass is valid for a single day's entry and is non-transferable.
  • Day pass fees are non-refundable, and day pass prices may change at any time.

5. CCTV & Privacy

  • All areas of the gym (except locker rooms and washrooms) are under CCTV surveillance for the safety of everyone in the gym. Management may review footage in the event of misconduct.
  • Fit Pit will keep your details private and will not share them with any third party without your consent, unless required by law. Your information is used only in connection with your visit and, if needed, to contact you or your emergency contact.
  • You may be asked to show photo ID at reception.

6. Local Conditions

  • Power outages are common in Zanzibar and beyond our control, despite the generators, UPS systems, and stabilisers we have in place. Fit Pit cannot be held responsible for any inconvenience, injury, or disruption caused by power fluctuations or outages.
